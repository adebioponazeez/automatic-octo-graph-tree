# GOLDEN · Good Content/Offer Piece — v30

> The standard for PRODUCT-CONTENT output.

## 1. Audience
Technical founders with a tight monthly AI budget who are tired of prompt rot and vendor lock-in.

## 2. Desired action
Sign up for a 7-day free trial of a "sovereign prompt OS" starter kit.

## 3. Offer / message (one sentence)
Keep your AI systems alive across models and years with files-first, vendor-neutral operating rules you fully control.

## 4. Deliverable
A 1-page landing section (headline, 3 proof-of-work bullets, 1 CTA) + a 5-line email intro.

**Headline:** "Your AI setup works today. Will it work in 7 years?"
**Bullets (verified claims only):**
- Your source of truth lives in files you own, not a chat.
- One job per operator — no god-agent, no overlap.
- Swap a model or vendor without rebuilding everything.
**CTA:** "Start the 7-day trial — no card required."

## 5. Why it might fail
- "7 years" feels abstract; the pain is today's prompt rot.
- Founders distrust another "system framework."
- No third-party testimonials exist yet → must lead with mechanism, not proof-by-social.

## 6. Repurposing notes
Same core can become a Twitter thread, a 2-min Loom, and a Reddit r/SaaS comment — all around the "survive model changes" hook.
