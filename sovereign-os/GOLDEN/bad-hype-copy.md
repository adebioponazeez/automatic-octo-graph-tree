# GOLDEN · Bad Example — Hype Copy — v30

> Recognizable symptom: invented results, testimonials, numbers, or false urgency.

**Tell-tale phrases:**
- "10,000 happy customers already love it!"
- "Thousands saved using our proven system."
- "Act now before this offer disappears forever!"
- "Industry-leading, world-class, #1 in the category."

**Why it's rejected (CRITIC lens):**
- Violates C2 (external truth) and the PRODUCT-CONTENT claim rules — no verified proof.
- Creates reputational debt and overpromise (lock-in to a claim you can't back).
- No META noting missing proof.

**Fix:** if proof is missing, say so and write around verified facts only. Swap invented numbers for "what we've shipped and can demonstrate."
